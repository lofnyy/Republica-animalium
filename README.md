# REPUBLICA ANIMALIUM

*When power becomes absolute, rebellion becomes inevitable.*

## About the Game

Republica Animalium is a dark narrative-driven adventure set in a world inhabited by animals, where power, wealth, and authority determine the value of a life.

Beneath the beauty of the forest lies a deeply corrupted society. Workers are exploited in dangerous mines, villagers are manipulated through fear and false beliefs, and the wealthy live comfortably at the expense of everyone else.

You play as a hedgehog whose life is torn apart by an oppressive system.

After surviving the brutal conditions of the mines, you return to find your village destroyed and your family gone. As you uncover the truth behind the tragedy, your journey takes you from the mines to the village, and eventually to the royal palace.

What begins as a struggle for survival becomes a fight against an entire system.

## Key Features

- **Story-driven adventure:** Experience a dark story told through three interconnected acts.
- **Boss battles:** Face powerful figures who represent different forms of oppression.
- **A world with meaning:** Explore environments that reflect inequality, corruption, and abuse of power.
- **Narrative-driven gameplay:** Discover the truth through dialogue, exploration, and environmental storytelling.
- **Two languages:** Play in English or Turkish.

## The Three Acts

**ACT I — The Mine**

A world where workers are treated as disposable tools. Challenge the authority that keeps them imprisoned.

**ACT II — The Village**

A devastated village, a manipulated population, and a truth hidden behind religious propaganda.

**ACT III — The Royal Palace**

Enter the heart of the kingdom. Confront the aristocracy and face the ruler responsible for your suffering.
